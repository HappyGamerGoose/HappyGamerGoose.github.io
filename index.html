About Me

Welcome to my academic homepage. I am a nursing educator and researcher dedicated to preparing the next generation of compassionate, evidence-based healthcare professionals.

My clinical background spans acute adult care and critical care simulation. My teaching focuses on clinical decision-making, patient advocacy, and integrating emerging healthcare technologies into clinical workflows.

Research & Scholarship

My research program examines high-fidelity simulation in nursing education, student clinical confidence, and interprofessional patient safety communication.
